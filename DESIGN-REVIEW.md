# Nillu Cafe — design and usability review

## Page journey

| Section | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening / liquid identity | Keep the supplied logo, one English headline, and the liquid name within the same opening. Include Kannada and Telugu scripts below it. | Establishes the cafe identity without making visitors pass through two introductory screens. English stays readable while regional scripts add character. |
| Our story | Keep a short explanation of the Kannada word “ನಿಲ್ಲು” and the invitation to pause. | Explains the name before the visual food journey. Avoids repeating the welcome copy. |
| Category navigation | Keep direct links to coffee, juice, bowls, desserts and quick snacks. | Visitors can choose a category instead of scrolling through every scene. |
| Coffee | Keep the approved beans → pour → filled cup sequence. | Familiar, clear opening to the product story. |
| Fresh juice | Keep fruit → blend → pour → glass; keep the base stationary during the pour. | Makes the transformation understandable and carries the interaction into a colorful category. |
| Smoothie bowls | Keep base → fruit → granola → finished bowl. | Shows a different action rather than repeating the drink scenes. |
| Desserts | Keep layers → cocoa → a lifted forkful. Use a product-focused bite rather than a human character. | Fits the stylized 3D approach and keeps attention on food. Tiramisu represents the category; text also names cheesecakes, custom cakes and 2D cakes. |
| Quick snacks | Keep this short and typographic. | Gives the visitor a visual rest after the larger animated scenes, while completing the offer. |
| Hospitality / services | Keep one dedicated section with three occasions: everyday coffee/snacks, fresh juice/bowls, and desserts/custom cakes. | Explains what the cafe offers and the intended welcome without inventing operational services. |
| Quality / hygiene | Keep two concise values, with Kannada companion words. | Communicates the priorities without claiming certifications, equipment or unconfirmed kitchen procedures. Hospitality has its own section, so it is not repeated here. |
| Closing | Keep a short invitation and a return-to-top link. | Gives the experience a calm ending. Add real visit details here when available. |

## Usability changes made

- Shortened the immersive sequences to reduce scroll fatigue.
- Added category navigation, chapter controls, and links to the next scene.
- Kept animation reversible through scrolling.
- Added a pause control for the repeating liquid lettering.
- Added small Kannada companion labels, with English carrying the core navigation and message.
- Used native scrolling for consistent chapter navigation.
- Reduced-motion visitors see finished products in shorter sections.
- Paused offscreen rendering for coffee, juice and the liquid identity. Bowl/dessert canvases mount near the viewport and have image fallbacks.
- Kept the logo and interface black and white; food supplies the color.

## What to add when confirmed

1. Real address, map link and opening hours. These are the most useful missing items for someone deciding to visit.
2. An actual cafe photograph, then real product photography when available. Generated interiors should not represent the real room.
3. A verified contact channel for custom cake enquiries, and any applicable lead time.
4. Confirmed practical details such as takeaway, dietary options and accessibility, if offered.
5. A native-language review of the Kannada/Telugu text before public launch.

No ordering, reservations, invented contact details, or unconfirmed facilities were added. This update is available locally and has not been published.

## Follow-up refinements applied

- Main navigation now uses “Our story,” “Food & drinks,” and “The Nillu way.” It returns on upward scrolling and remains available when keyboard-focused.
- The opening has a text-labelled food-and-drinks link alongside the arrow.
- Visitors can explicitly skip all animated food scenes to the quick snacks and cafe sections.
- The explanation of Nillu is shorter and avoids repeating the same invitation.
- Removed an extra caption under the liquid name to simplify the opening.
- Increased mobile chapter-control and liquid-pause tap areas.
- Softened dessert edges and added procedural surface texture to the cake layers.

The address, opening hours, verified cake enquiry channel, actual hospitality services and real interior photos remain content inputs, not invented sections.

## Latest layout decisions

The individual food scenes no longer repeat website headers, logos or story links. One main navigation serves the full page; one compact category chooser contains the optional scene-skip link.

At the owner's request, Hospitality is included again as a third value alongside Quality and Hygiene, with the Kannada label ಆತಿಥ್ಯ. This short statement complements the dedicated hospitality section: “A warm hello. An attentive ear. Our intention is to make you feel welcome, from the first greeting to the next visit.”
