OUR STORY / A WORLD IN TWO DIMENSIONS

Our name means stop.
Our space gives you a reason.

“ನಿಲ್ಲು” means “stop” in Kannada. We’re bringing that invitation to life through an illustrated 2D setting, food to look forward to, and a warm welcome. Come for a coffee, a catch-up, or a celebration of your own.
