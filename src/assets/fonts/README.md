Self-hosted fonts from Google Fonts (https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl). Manrope and Fraunces are the approved site pairing. Outfit and Noto Sans Kannada/Telugu preserve the liquid identity; Jost and Cormorant Garamond are used only by the comparison preview. Each family includes its original SIL Open Font License. Download with python3 scripts/download-site-fonts.py.

WOFF2 copies are used by the site to reduce transfer sizes. Latin faces are subset to Latin/extended Latin, punctuation, currency, trademark and arrow symbols. Kannada and Telugu faces retain complete glyph and shaping coverage. After downloading the original TTFs, run `python scripts/compress-fonts.py` in an environment with `fonttools[woff]` installed. Original TTFs and licenses are retained.
